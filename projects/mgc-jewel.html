FAQs

Everything you need to know.

Is MGC Jewel RDA approved?

Yes. MGC Jewel is the first RDA (Rawalpindi Development Authority)-approved 300-foot highrise in Bahria Town — a published MGC claim confirming the project meets government-mandated legal, structural and urban planning requirements.

What residential units are available?

1-bedroom and 2-bedroom apartments, Jacuzzi apartments, beach-view apartments and penthouses — all with high-end amenities and premium finishing.

What is the Cultural Avenue?

A dedicated commercial concept within Jewel's mall giving businesses from Pakistan's four regional cultures dedicated space to showcase their products, crafts and brands with mainstream footfall.

How much parking is there?

Three basement levels of digital car parking with capacity for 700 vehicles, integrating technology for entry, exit and space management.
