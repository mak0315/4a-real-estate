El Casa by MGC · Overview

Airport-side living with Margalla light.

Mumtaz City's position beside the motorways and the CPEC route is the whole thesis — El Casa is MGC's residential expression of it. A G+15 highrise of curated apartments whose skyline faces the Margalla Hills, roughly 10–15 minutes from Islamabad International Airport.
