Flexible Payment Options

Own property. Pay smarter.

MGC Developments pioneered Pakistan's 1% monthly payment plan in 2023 — and it's the plan most clients ask about first. Here's the shape of it, in three steps. Every project is different, so WhatsApp is where the real numbers live.

01

Book with a down payment

Lock your unit with a down payment (typically 20%) on selected projects.

02

Pay 1% every month

1% of the total property value per month — no interest, on MGC's signature plan.

03

Live in it — or let it earn

On certain plans you can live, rent or earn while you pay — subject to project terms.

Payment options are set by MGC Developments and apply to selected projects. Terms — including down payment, possession and rental deduction policies — vary by project and current offer. Investment decisions carry risk and no guaranteed returns or appreciation are implied. Confirm your project's terms with Ayan.
