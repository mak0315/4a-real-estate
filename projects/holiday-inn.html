Holiday Inn & Suites · Overview

An IHG landmark for the twin cities.

MGC Developments signed an IHG franchise to bring the Holiday Inn brand to Islamabad — the city's first 4-star Holiday Inn. The 150-key property is planned on the main GT Road at DHA Phase II, the most travelled corridor between Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

The Property

Hospitality with a global name.

150 Keys

Rooms and suites under the Holiday Inn brand, operated to IHG's international standards.

The Opportunity

An internationally branded hotel asset on Pakistan's busiest intercity road — a hospitality thesis with a decades-long compounding horizon.

Location

GT Road · DHA Phase II

Planned on the main GT Road at DHA Phase II — the bridge corridor between Islamabad and Rawalpindi, carrying daily intercity traffic past airport approaches, corporate Islamabad and the country's largest private housing societies. It is the site that ties the portfolio's Bahria Town and Mumtaz City addresses together.
